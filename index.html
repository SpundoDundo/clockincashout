Our Story

Three friends.
One goal.

Clock In, Cash Out WY was started by three friends with one goal — to help Wyoming youth find jobs and learn the basics of managing their money. We provide free resume building, interview prep, and financial education covering topics like reading your first paycheck, budgeting, saving, and understanding 401(k)s and compound interest.

We won't tell you what to do with your money — we'll make sure you understand it. We worked throughout high school averaging 20–35 hours a week across multiple jobs while balancing school. Nobody taught us the rights and wrongs, so we want to make that easier for everyone else.
